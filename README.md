# Aarohi – Girl Education Empowerment Platform


Aarohi is a multilingual educational empowerment platform designed to support girls after completing their 10th grade by providing easy access to career guidance, scholarships, government schemes, learning resources, internships, and educational opportunities. The platform brings essential educational and career-related information together in one place, helping students make informed decisions about their academic journey and future careers.

Aarohi aims to bridge the information gap that often prevents students from discovering suitable educational pathways and financial support. By organizing relevant opportunities and guidance into accessible sections, the platform helps students explore their interests, understand available options, and take meaningful steps toward achieving their educational and professional goals.

## Key Features and Functionality

### 1. Career Guidance and Educational Roadmaps

Provides guidance to help students explore career options after 10th grade and understand the different academic pathways available to them. Students can learn about higher education opportunities, potential career choices, and the steps required to pursue their interests. The platform aims to simplify career planning by helping students identify suitable directions and understand the qualifications and skills needed for their chosen paths.

### 2. Scholarships and Educational Opportunities

Brings together information about scholarships, internships, fellowships, and educational programs that can help students continue their education and develop professionally. This feature helps students discover potential financial assistance and opportunities to gain practical experience, pursue their interests, and strengthen their academic profiles. By making these opportunities easier to explore, Aarohi encourages students to seek resources that support their growth.

### 3. Government Schemes and Financial Assistance

Provides information about government initiatives focused on women's education, financial assistance, skill development, and empowerment. This section helps students discover programs that may support their educational expenses, improve access to training, or create pathways toward greater independence. It aims to make relevant schemes easier to understand and explore so students can identify opportunities that may be applicable to them.

### 4. Learning Resources and Skill Development

Offers access to learning resources that encourage students to build academic, technical, and professional skills. These include programming resources, communication skills, certifications, and other skill-development opportunities. The feature supports continuous learning beyond the classroom and helps students explore areas of interest, strengthen their capabilities, and prepare for future academic or employment opportunities.

### 5. Educational Events and Career-Building Activities

Helps students discover educational workshops, seminars, competitions, and career-related events that can contribute to their personal and professional development. Such activities can expose students to new fields, practical knowledge, and learning experiences beyond their regular curriculum. This feature encourages active participation in educational communities and helps students identify opportunities to expand their knowledge and interests.

### 6. Multilingual Accessibility

Supports English, Telugu, and Hindi to make educational information accessible to students from different linguistic backgrounds. By allowing students to engage with the platform in a language they understand, Aarohi reduces language barriers and makes career guidance and educational opportunities easier to explore. This feature is particularly valuable for reaching students who may find it difficult to navigate educational information available only in English.

### 7. User Registration and Login

Provides user registration and login functionality, allowing students to access the platform through a structured user experience. Authentication establishes a foundation for a more personalized educational platform and supports the development of additional student-focused features in the future.

## Real-World Impact

Aarohi addresses a practical challenge faced by many students: finding reliable and relevant information about education, career pathways, financial assistance, and skill development can be difficult when resources are scattered across different platforms. By bringing these categories together, Aarohi simplifies the process of discovering opportunities and helps students make more informed decisions about their futures.

The platform is designed to promote educational awareness, encourage career exploration, improve access to information about financial support, and empower girls to pursue their ambitions with greater confidence. Its multilingual approach further supports inclusivity by making essential information accessible to a wider range of students.

## Technologies Used

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React.js, Vite, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB
